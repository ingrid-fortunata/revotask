# RevoTask User Guide · Draft

Verify these labels and steps against the live app before submission. Link the verified deployment in the root README.

1. Register with a name, an email and a password of 8 to 10 characters. The password needs an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter and a special character such as `!`, `?`, `#` or `%`. Then sign in. The dashboard lists boards you can access.
2. Create a board as its admin. In Create a board you can also set a sprint with the optional Sprint start and Sprint end dates (both or neither, at most 90 days). An admin can change or clear the dates later with Edit board (Clear dates, then Save changes). The sprint shows under the board name. Use the Members control to add a registered teammate by email. Add workflow columns, and flag one empty column as Done if you want completion reporting.
3. Add a card to a column. Click its title (**Open**) to see Card details. Edit the title or description, assign a participant and post a comment. Only you can edit or remove your own comments. If a comment may not have posted because the connection dropped, the dialog says so and shows the refreshed list before you try again.
4. Drag a card on desktop or use its **Move** control with keyboard or touch. After moving it, reload to verify the new position. If a move fails, look for feedback and retry after the board refreshes.
5. On a board, type in **Search cards** to find a card by title (results appear after a short pause and show the card's column). Select a result to open its details. Open **My tasks** from the header (a second row on a phone) to see your assignments across accessible boards, grouped by board. Use the notification bell in the header (the count shows how many updates you have not read) to open your notifications. You get one when someone assigns you a card or comments on a card assigned to you. Select a notification to open its board, which also marks it read, or use **Mark as read**. Use **Newer** and **Older** to page through them. The count refreshes about every 30 seconds.
6. Press Analytics at the right end of the board header. A dialog opens with the board's sprint (for example "Sprint 16 Nov to 22 Nov", the start day to the end day in UTC, with no time shown) or, when the board has no sprint, the current week (Monday to Sunday UTC, with its start and end shown in your local time), the cards completed this sprint or this week (each card counts once), the cards in each column and each member's activity this sprint or this week (cards created or moved, columns reordered and comments). It updates after your own card or column changes. Press Refresh to see comments and other people's changes. If it cannot load, the panel says so and offers Retry, and the board keeps working. Select your name in the header to open Profile, where you can change your name or email (Save changes stays off until you change something). A taken email shows an error under the field. Your session stays signed in after an email change, and you sign in with the new email next time. Password changes are not available yet. Sign out from the header.
7. Open your profile from your name in the header (top bar or sidebar). Change your name and press **Save changes**. Your email is shown but cannot be changed, because you sign in with it. To change your password, press **Change password**, enter your current password and a new one (8 to 10 characters with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter and a special character) and save. A wrong current password shows an error under that field and changes nothing. Other sessions stay signed in after a password change, and there is no password reset, so keep the new password safe. Press **Cancel password change** to close the fields without sending anything.
