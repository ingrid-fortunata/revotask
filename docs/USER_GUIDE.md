# RevoTask User Guide · Draft

Verify these labels and steps against the live app before submission. Link the verified deployment in the root README.

1. Register with a name, an email and a password of 8 to 128 characters. The password needs an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter and a special character such as `!`, `?`, `#` or `%`. Then sign in. The dashboard lists boards you can access.
2. Create a board as its admin. Use the Members control to add a registered teammate by email. Add workflow columns, and flag one empty column as Done if you want completion reporting.
3. Add a card to a column. Click its title (**Open**) to see Card details. Edit the title or description, assign a participant and post a comment. Only you can edit or remove your own comments. If a comment may not have posted because the connection dropped, the dialog says so and shows the refreshed list before you try again.
4. Drag a card on desktop or use its **Move** control with keyboard or touch. After moving it, reload to verify the new position. If a move fails, look for feedback and retry after the board refreshes.
5. On a board, type in **Search cards** to find a card by title (results appear after a short pause and show the card's column). Select a result to open its details. Open **My tasks** from the header (a second row on a phone) to see your assignments across accessible boards, grouped by board. Use the notification bell to read your updates.
6. Open board analytics for cards per column, distinct cards completed this UTC week and weekly activity. Select your name in the header to open Profile, where you can change your name or email (Save changes stays off until you change something). A taken email shows an error under the field. Your session stays signed in after an email change, and you sign in with the new email next time. Password changes are not available yet. Sign out from the header.
